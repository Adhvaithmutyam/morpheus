TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION FOR CENTRIFUGAL WATER PUMPING UNIT

1.0 SCOPE AND OPERATING CONDITIONS
3.1 The centrifugal pump shall deliver water with minimum operating pressure of 10 bar.
3.2 All pumps shall be tested for performance as per IS 1520 : 2007.

2.0 ELECTRICAL MOTOR DRIVER
3.3 The motor shall be a three phase induction motor rated for 415 V, 50 Hz power supply.

3.0 MATERIAL AND QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
3.4 The pump casing shall be constructed of cast iron.
3.5 The equipment shall carry valid ISI mark and BIS certification.
